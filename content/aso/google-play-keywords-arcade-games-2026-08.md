Arcade returns forty-six open terms out of thirty-one thousand keywords, and
thirty-four of them score under 30 on [Rankgrip](/aso/rankgrip/). Sixteen appear
in no other report this month — the second most distinct issue in the August set,
after simulation.

The number worth stopping on is a different one.

## One app is number one for sixteen of the forty-six

`Aippy: créer un jeu` currently holds the top slot for sixteen of these
keywords — better than a third of the report. `Sekai : Créer · Remix · Jouer`
holds three more and `1000 Offline Games in 1` another three.

None of the three is an arcade game. Aippy and Sekai generate games; the third is
a compilation. Between them they hold the top slot for twenty-two of the
forty-six open arcade terms — and they got there without shipping an arcade game
at all.

`ai apps that make games` at Rankgrip 29 is the honest version of that query.
`verity mode` at 29, held by a Minecraft mods app, is the same phenomenon wearing
a different hat: a search that sounds like a game and returns a utility.

The reading for a studio is practical. **A slot held by a tool is a slot held by
something with no reason to defend it.** Those apps rank on generic vocabulary
they happen to contain, not on relevance any player would recognise — so a real
arcade game carrying the phrase in its listing has a better claim than the
numbers alone suggest.

## The reachable terms

Below the tool cluster the arcade rows look like arcade. `chicken roll game` has
the lowest grip in the report at 7, with a thirteen-month-old leader. `boom chip
clash` sits at 14 with a nine-month-old app on it. `game chicken road` is at 16
and `spinner merge master` at 34 with real demand of 70.

`gold merge earn money` at 25 also appears in the simulation report. Merge
mechanics list across both categories, so the term does too.

## The wall, and one hole in it

`block blast` at Rankgrip 68 and `place blocks` at 47 are both held by Block
Blast!, an app past a billion installs. `single player` sits at 67 and `offline
play` at 59.

`place blocks` is the anomaly again, the same one the puzzle report found: a
billion-install app at number one, seven of the ten held by big developers, and
the score still comes in at 47 — because not one app carries the phrase in its
title. None of that makes it easy. It does make it a different fight from
`block blast` at 68, where the title match is the thing in your way.

## The phrases are short here

Arcade leans shorter than the other categories this month. Its terms are mostly
two and three words, where simulation runs to four and five. Arcade play is
easier to name than it is to describe, so people search for the object rather
than the situation: a chicken, a spinner, a chip.

Short phrases are usually the ones already gone, so a short one still open is
worth more attention than its Rankgrip alone implies. `boom chip clash` at 14:
three words, a nine-month-old leader, and nothing sitting between it and whoever
wants it.

## What this issue shares

Twenty-three of these rows also appear in the casual report and seventeen in
puzzle. Arcade games are routinely listed under both, so their keywords are. The
measurement is the same in each; only the surrounding category differs.
