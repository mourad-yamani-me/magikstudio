Simulation is the deepest category measured so far. A hundred and ten terms
survived the gate, a hundred and one are printed, and seventy-four of those
score under 30 on [Rankgrip](/aso/rankgrip/) — genuinely open, more than in any
other August issue. It is also the least borrowed: fifty-nine of its rows appear
in no other report this month, where casual manages eight.

Then you look at who holds them.

## One app holds twenty-nine of the hundred and one

`AIKO: AI Girlfriend 3D Game` is the current number one for twenty-nine terms in
this report. Add `Blush: AI Dating Simulator` at nine and `Intimate` at five and
a single product category holds forty-three of the hundred and one open rows.

The scores are real. `ai anime girl 3d` has a Rankgrip of 7, `ai 3d girl` an 8,
`ai girlfriend simulator game escape` a 10. Nobody large is contesting them and
no app carries the phrase in its title. By every number this report prints, they
are the most takeable terms in Play simulation.

They are also a market most studios cannot enter and should not want to. Worth
saying out loud, because a reader skimming for the lowest Rankgrip finds this
cluster first — and it tells them nothing useful about the simulation genre they
actually work in.

Strip the AI companion apps out and the picture changes shape. What remains is
`india bike game logo` at 27, `1 speed keyboard escape roblox` at 29, `3d hide
and seek painting game` at 25, `gold merge earn money` at 25 — driving, obstacle
courses, hidden object, merge. Those are the reachable simulation terms, and
there are around sixty of them.

## The phrases are long here

Thirty-two of the hundred and one are four words and seventeen are five, with
another ten running to six or seven. Two-word phrases account for twelve. No
other category this month leans so far into the long tail.

That follows from how people search for simulators. Nobody types "simulator":
they type what they want to simulate, and under what conditions. `1 speed
keyboard escape candy chocolate` is a real query, Rankgrip 9, with a four-month-
old app sitting on it. Phrases like that are unappealing to write into a listing.
They are also exactly where an unknown app can still be found.

## The wall

`satisfying` tops it again at Rankgrip 80, as it did in puzzle, and `merge`
follows at 74 — one word, held by an app with half a million installs and enough
title matches to make the phrase theirs. `tricky game brain quiz offline` sits at
51 with Brain Test on it at a hundred million installs.

The interesting one is `aisle secrets merge the drama offline` at 53: six words,
plainly a search for one specific game, and walled anyway. Length alone buys you
nothing.

## What this issue shares

Thirty-three of these rows also appear in the casual report and twelve in
puzzle. A keyword takes the categories of the apps holding its top ten, and a
merge game listed as both casual and simulation puts its terms in both. Where a
row shows up twice, the numbers are the same measurement, not two.
