1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how PerfectMatch: Numbers (developed by Indie Core Dev) handles your information when you use our game. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe experience for all users.

2. Personal Information

We do not collect or store any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, location, or device-specific data.

9. Offline Use

You can enjoy PerfectMatch: Numbers offline without any data collection or internet connection.

10. Data Sharing

We do not share or sell your data with any third parties. Any data collected by third-party services (like AdMob or Firebase) is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Since we do not store user data, requests for data access or deletion are not applicable. However, if you have any concerns, you may still contact us.
