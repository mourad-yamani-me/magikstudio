Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 17-06-2026

Last Updated: 17-06-2026

Package ID: com.logoquiz.guessbrand

Contact Email: [email&#160;protected]

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Logo Quiz: Guess Brand (developed by Indie Core Dev) handles your information when you use our game. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe experience for all users.

2. Personal Information

We do not collect or store any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. We do not ask you to create an account or provide any personal details to play.

3. Cloud Save & Game Progress

Logo Quiz: Guess Brand offers an optional Cloud Save feature so your game progress (such as solved levels, coins, and settings) can be synced and restored. This feature uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication together with Cloud Firestore.

How this works:

  - When Cloud Save is active, the app signs you in anonymously. Firebase generates a random, anonymous user ID that is not linked to your name, email, or any personal identity.

  - Your game progress data is stored in Firestore under this anonymous ID. No personal information is included in this data.

  - This data is used only to restore your progress on the device and is never used for advertising, profiling, or tracking.

  - You may request deletion of this data at any time (see Section 14).

4. Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with our game. This data helps us improve gameplay and fix bugs. Firebase may collect anonymized usage data such as session length, crash events, and interaction patterns.

For more details, see the Firebase Privacy and Security page.

5. Crash Reporting

We use Firebase Crashlytics to monitor, log, and analyze crash reports. This helps us maintain app stability and improve performance. Data collected is anonymized and does not contain personal identifiers.

6. Third-Party Services & Ads

Our game displays advertisements using the following ad provider:

  - AdMob (by Google)

These ad networks may use device identifiers or cookies to show you relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized ads via your device settings.

The third-party services we rely on (Google AdMob and Firebase) are governed by their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the Google Privacy Policy for more information.

7. Notifications

Logo Quiz: Guess Brand may send you local notifications (for example, gentle reminders to come back and play). These notifications are scheduled and displayed entirely on your device. We do not use push notification tokens, and no notification data is sent to or stored on any server we own. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

8. User Accounts

Logo Quiz: Guess Brand does not require account creation or login. The Cloud Save feature described in Section 3 uses anonymous authentication only and never requires a username, password, or email address.

9. In-App Purchases

Our game does not offer in-app purchases (IAPs).

10. Children's Privacy

Our game is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from users in this age group. If you believe we have unknowingly collected personal information from a child, please contact us at [email&#160;protected].

11. Offline Use

You can enjoy Logo Quiz: Guess Brand offline without an internet connection. Ads, Firebase Analytics, crash reporting, and Cloud Save syncing require an internet connection. When offline, your progress is saved locally on your device and synced the next time you are online (if Cloud Save is enabled).

12. Data Sharing

We do not share or sell your data with any third parties. Any data collected by third-party services (like AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or Cloud Firestore) is governed by their respective privacy policies.

13. Data Storage & Retention

We do not store any personally identifiable information on our own servers. Anonymous Cloud Save data (game progress tied to a random anonymous ID) is retained in Cloud Firestore for as long as the Cloud Save feature is in use, so that your progress can be restored. You may request its deletion at any time.

14. Your Rights

Because Cloud Save data is tied only to an anonymous identifier and contains no personal information, it cannot be used to identify you. If you would like your Cloud Save data deleted, or if you have any concerns about how your information is handled, please contact us at [email&#160;protected] and we will assist you.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

16. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact:
